Why most phone videos feel flat

You have a phone. Now learn what you can do with it.

Maybe you have watched videos online and wondered how people get such smooth, captivating visuals from nothing more than a phone. Maybe you have tried editing your own videos and they still do not look the way you pictured them. Or maybe you have thought about becoming a videographer, but the cost of expensive equipment held you back.

Your phone can do more than you realize. But having a good phone is not the same as knowing how to use it.

What separates average footage from work people stop scrolling for is a process. Camera settings, composition, movement, storytelling, editing, sound and colour all play a part, and so does knowing how to approach a shoot and present your work to clients. That is exactly what I teach in this course, from the first shot to the final edit.

The Curriculum

Everything you need to go from your first shot to your final edit, in the order you will use it.

Eight practical modules built around demonstrations and step-by-step application, plus a set of bonuses, so you always know what to do next with your phone in your hand.

01Introduction to Mobile Videography

Start with the fundamentals. Before you can shoot better, you need to understand the camera in your hand and how to get the most from it.

  • Camera settings for mobile videography
  • Frame rates explained
  • Essential accessories for beginners
02Shooting Like a Professional

I show you how to make your footage more intentional, more engaging and far better to look at.

  • Camera angles and composition
  • The rule of thirds and framing
  • Smooth camera movements
  • Storytelling through videos
03Editing in CapCut

I walk you through practical editing techniques in CapCut, step by step, so you can turn raw footage into polished content.

  • Introduction to CapCut
  • Cutting and trimming clips
  • Transitions and overlays
  • The cloning effect
  • Keyframes
  • Text and effects
  • The recap effect
  • Export settings
04Content Creation & Storytelling

A good video begins before you press record. I show you how to prepare for a shoot, understand what your client wants and capture the footage that tells the right story.

  • How to plan for a shoot
  • How to understand a client's vision
  • How to avoid missing important moments
  • A bonus practical tip for your shooting process
05Sound Design

Visuals matter, and sound matters just as much. I teach you to use audio to make your videos feel complete.

  • Introduction to sound design
  • Selecting the right music
  • Extracting and cutting sound
  • Creating voiceovers
  • Using sound effects
  • Layering sounds
06Colour Grading

I show you how to give your footage a more polished, consistent look that makes your work recognisable.

  • What colour grading means
  • How to colour grade your videos
  • Skin-tone balancing
07Event & Wedding Videography
